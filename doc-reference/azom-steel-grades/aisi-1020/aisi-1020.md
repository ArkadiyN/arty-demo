# AISI 1020 Steel

## Overview

AISI 1020 is a low-carbon steel recognized for its excellent machinability, ductility, and weldability. It is among the most commonly used steels in industrial applications, suitable for applications requiring ease of machining and forming.

## Chemical Composition

| Element        | Min (%) | Max (%) |
| -------------- | ------- | ------- |
| Carbon (C)     | 0.17    | 0.23    |
| Iron (Fe)      | 99.08   | 99.53   |
| Manganese (Mn) | 0.30    | 0.60    |
| Phosphorus (P) | —       | 0.040   |
| Sulfur (S)     | —       | 0.050   |

## Mechanical Properties

### Strength

- **Yield Strength (YS):** 294.74 MPa (42,748 psi)
- **Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS):** 394.72 MPa (57,249 psi)

### Ductility

- **Elongation at Break:** 36.5%
- **Reduction of Area:** 66.0%

### Hardness

- **Brinell Hardness (HB):** 111

### Toughness & Impact

- **Charpy Impact:** 16.9–68.0 J (temperature-dependent)
- **Izod Impact:** 125 J

### Elastic Properties

- **Modulus of Elasticity (E):** 200 GPa (29,000 ksi)

## Physical Properties

- **Density:** 7.87 g/cc

## Thermal Properties

(Not specified in source)

## Characteristics

- **High machinability**
- **High strength** (relative to other low-carbon steels)
- **High ductility**
- **Good weldability**

### Hardening & Strengthening

- **Induction Hardening:** Not recommended (low carbon content)
- **Flame Hardening:** Not recommended (low carbon content)
- **Carburization:** Responds well; achieves Rc ≥ 65 after case hardening

## Applications

AISI 1020 is widely used for:

- Shafts
- Gears
- Fasteners (bolts, studs, rivets)
- General structural components
- Machinery parts requiring good machinability and ductility
