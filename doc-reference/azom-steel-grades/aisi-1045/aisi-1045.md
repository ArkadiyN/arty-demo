# AISI 1045 Steel

## Overview

AISI 1045 is a medium-carbon steel widely used for machinery, structural components, and applications requiring moderate strength and decent machinability. It offers a balance between strength and workability.

| Iron (Fe)      | 98.51   | 98.98   |
| Manganese (Mn) | 0.60    | 0.90    |
| Phosphorus (P) | —       | 0.040   |
| Sulfur (S)     | —       | 0.050   |

## Mechanical Properties

### Strength

- **Yield Strength (YS):** 310 MPa (45000 psi)
- **Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS):** 565 MPa (81900 psi)

### Ductility

- **Elongation at Break:** 16.0%
- **Reduction of Area:** 40.0%

### Hardness

- **Brinell Hardness (HB):** 163

### Elastic Properties

- **Modulus of Elasticity (E):** 200 GPa (29000 ksi)
- **Shear Modulus (G):** 80 GPa (11600 ksi)
- **Poisson's Ratio (ν):** 0.290

## Physical Properties

- **Density:** 7.87 g/cc (0.284 lb/in³)
