# AISI 1045 Steel — Card

**Source:** AZoM article (ArticleID=6130)

## Composition

- **Carbon:** 0.42–0.50% (matches the ~0.45% baseline noted in fragmentation.py)
- **Manganese:** 0.60–0.90%
- **Silicon:** not specified
- **Phosphorus:** ≤0.040%
- **Sulfur:** ≤0.050%
- **Iron:** balance (98.51–98.98%)

## Mechanical Properties (§ Mechanical Properties)

- **Yield Strength (YS):** **310 MPa** or **45000 psi** (§ Mechanical Properties) — *exact match to "45000 psi" cited in fragmentation catalog*
- **Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS):** 565 MPa or 81900 psi
- **Hardness (Brinell):** 163 HB
- **Elongation at Break:** 16.0%
- **Reduction of Area:** 40.0%
- **Modulus of Elasticity:** 200 GPa (29000 ksi)
- **Shear Modulus:** 80 GPa (11600 ksi)
- **Poisson's Ratio:** 0.29

## Physical Properties

- **Density:** 7.87 g/cc (0.284 lb/in³)

## Applications & Notes

- Medium-carbon steel for general machinery and structural parts
- **War Department (WD) nomenclature:** NOT mentioned in source

**This is likely the source of the "45000 psi" baseline figure in the catalog.** %C range matches the 0.45% noted.
