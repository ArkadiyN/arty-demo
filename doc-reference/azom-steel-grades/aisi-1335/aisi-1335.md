# AISI 1335 Alloy Steel (UNS G13350)

## Overview

AISI 1335 is a medium-carbon alloy steel commonly used in machined components. It is classified as a medium-manganese, medium-carbon steel suitable for applications requiring moderate strength and machinability.

## Chemical Composition

| Element        | Min (%) | Max (%) |
| -------------- | ------- | ------- |
| Carbon (C)     | 0.33    | 0.38    |
| Iron (Fe)      | 97.30   | 97.92   |
| Manganese (Mn) | 1.60    | 1.90    |
| Silicon (Si)   | 0.15    | 0.35    |
| Phosphorus (P) | —       | 0.035   |
| Sulfur (S)     | —       | 0.040   |

## Mechanical Properties

### Hardness

- **Brinell Hardness (HB):** 179–235
- **Rockwell Hardness (HRc):** 15

### Elastic Properties

- **Elastic Modulus (E):** 190–210 GPa
- **Bulk Modulus:** 140 GPa
- **Shear Modulus (G):** 80 GPa

### Machinability

- **Machinability Rating:** 55 (relative to AISI 1212 at 100)

**Note:** Yield strength and ultimate tensile strength values are not provided in the source document.

## Physical Properties

- **Density:** 7.87 g/cm³
- **Specific Heat Capacity:** (not specified)

## Thermal Properties

- **Coefficient of Thermal Expansion:** 11.5 μm/(m·K)
- **Thermal Conductivity:** 51.9 W/(m·K)

## Applications

AISI 1335 is primarily used in **machined components** where moderate strength and good machinability are required. The medium-manganese content provides improved hardenability compared to plain carbon steels.
