# AISI 1335 Alloy Steel (UNS G13350) — Card

**Source:** AZoM article (ArticleID=6667)

## Composition

- **Carbon:** 0.33–0.38% (not the ~0.45% of SAE 1045; lower carbon content)
- **Manganese:** 1.60–1.90%
- **Silicon:** 0.15–0.35%
- **Phosphorus:** ≤0.035%
- **Sulfur:** ≤0.040%
- **Iron:** balance (97.3–97.92%)

## Mechanical Properties (§ Mechanical Properties)

- **Hardness (Brinell):** 179–235 HB
- **Hardness (Rockwell C):** 15 HRc
- **Elastic Modulus:** 190–210 GPa
- **Shear Modulus:** 80 GPa
- **Machinability:** 55 (relative to AISI 1212 = 100)

**Yield Strength & UTS:** Not provided in source

## Physical Properties

- **Density:** 7.87 g/cm³
- **Thermal Expansion:** 11.5 μm/(m·K)
- **Thermal Conductivity:** 51.9 W/(m·K)

## Application & Notes

- Primary use: machined components
