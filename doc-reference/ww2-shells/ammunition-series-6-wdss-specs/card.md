## Chemical Trends and Design Classification

### Carbon Content Strategy

- **WDSS 1:** Low-carbon (0.14–0.20%) — minimized strength, maximum ductility for thin-walled mortar shells
- **WDSS 2:** Medium-low (0.28–0.34%) — modest hardening over WDSS 1; retains forgeability
- **WDSS 3, 5, 6, 7:** Carbon maxima (0.60–0.65%) — progressive hardening, reduced ductility; likely for projectile body strength

### Manganese and Silicon Hardening

- **WDSS 1:** High Mn (1.00–1.30%) — substitutional hardening to offset low C
- **WDSS 2–7:** Reduced Mn (≤1.00%) or equal; increased Si (0.15–0.30% vs. ≤0.10%) — Si provides precipitation hardening and solid-solution strengthening
- **WDSS 7:** Highest Mn ceiling (≤1.30%) — possibly for lower-C variants requiring additional hardening

### Impurity Control (P, S)

- **WDSS 1:** Elevated sulfur (0.08–0.13%) — unusual (typical specs ≤0.05%); possible wartime relaxation or MnS inclusion control
- **WDSS 2–7:** Strict P, S limits (≤0.040%, ≤0.050%) — higher purity typical of post-war standards
